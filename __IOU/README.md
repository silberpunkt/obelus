# IOU

What Obelus owes back to the work it stands on.

The idea is User1's, 15 November 2026: "I had the concept of an __IOU, over the usual expectations of a PR. In my mind, we 'owe' Ecouter all our innovations back. We could maintain a folder of IOUs and name ecouter, containing the details and samples if any... So it becomes, not a messageboard, but a record of giving-back and a notebox of thank-you notes. That box could be a __paid folder, where closed IOUs may remain or be excused."

A pull request says: take my change, as I wrote it, now. An IOU says: here is what we learned by using your work; it is yours when it suits you. It asks nothing of the creditor's timing or form, and it keeps the record whether or not anything is ever merged.

## The form

One folder for each creditor, one file for each debt. Every IOU has four parts:

- **What we took.** What of theirs we used.
- **What we made of it.** What was done here, and why.
- **What we owe back.** The smallest change that would carry it home, and where in Obelus it lives. Samples beside it, if any.
- **How to tell it is paid.**

Each is checked against the creditor's own source before it is written, and says which version it was checked against. An IOU should name something the creditor can use. "Everything we did" is not a debt.

## Paid

When a debt is settled its file moves to `__paid`, with one more line: how it was settled, or that it was excused, and why. "The Why helps all readers better understand what passes." An excused debt is kept too: it says the thing was considered.

## Open

Nothing is owed. Every debt Obelus has written down so far is paid, and at rest in [`__paid`](__paid): to **Ecouter** (https://github.com/silberpunkt/ecouter), the tape and its head (IOU 1), the three things done to the score (IOU 2), the voices found by ear (IOU 3) and the room after the head (IOU 4), all on 18 November 2026.
