# IOU 3: Three voices found by ear

To Ecouter, from Obelus. 15 November 2026. Checked against Ecouter 4c01db6.

## What we took

Ecouter has presets for its finish and no notion of a voice. The voices came to Obelus by way of Nuage, which keeps
them as a small table of operations upon Ecouter: pace, pitch, throat, breath, hiss, and how pitch and pace arc across
a sentence. So this debt is partly Nuage's to collect.

## What we made of it

Three voices, each found by User1 by ear and none by design, and a fourth retuned:

| Voice | What it is | Tone | Speed | Song | Steady |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Obelus | Arthur, with the tape run at about four fifths | 1.03 | 0.83 | 0.53 | |
| Rudolf | | 0.96 | 0.98 | 0.61 | |
| Lauren | Nuage's own, with breath 1.5, pitch arc 0.04, hiss 0.9 | 0.86 | 0.96 | 0.43 | 0.65 |
| Miria | Nuage's Miria, retuned | 1.03 | 1.04 | 0.79 | 0.87 |

And one finding. Obelus was discovered by throwing the line a little slow. User1: "A warm, slow, fuller voice than I
have heard Ecouter to be capable... It was entirely about play speed." His recording measured 104.6 Hz against
Arthur's 130.1 at the spoken pace. So a voice may be found with nothing but the speed of the tape, and then kept.

Each voice remembers its own Tone, Speed, Song and Steady.

## What we owe back

The four rows above. And a proposal: that Ecouter carry a table of voices of its own, so that a voice is asked for by
name and is not declared again in every page that speaks.

## How to tell it is paid

These four can be asked for by name from Ecouter, or from Nuage if that is judged the right home; and Obelus's own
copy of the table is gone.

## Paid

18 November 2026, by Ecouter's commit a0c8d79.

Ecouter now carries a table of voices of its own, in its score ring (`ecouter-score.js`, as `ECOUTER.SCORE.VOICES`).
There are eight, and each is asked for by name: Phillippe, who is stock Ecouter, and Nuage, Miria, Lauren, Arthur,
Obelus, Rudolf and Blando. A voice is an arc laid over a sentence and four dials. The arcs are Nuage's, taken from her
note "Voices upon Ecouter" and held against her own operation in Ecouter's test: equal to the last bit. The four rows
above went in as they stand, for Tone, Speed and Steady.

Song went in differently, by User1's ruling of the same day. He found that song belongs to the voices outside Obelus
too, "though less musically than in Obelus", and set a number for each: Miria 0.63, Rudolf 0.61, Obelus and Arthur
0.53, Phillippe, Nuage and Lauren 0.34, Blando 0.28. Obelus sings at his own 0.53 everywhere, so that the voice played
elsewhere sounds like the one known here. Of the rest he says: "In truth these are guesses and I need my ears too."

How it was told to be paid. Obelus's table of voices and its arc are gone, and the four rows of dials with them. The
page carries Ecouter's module and asks it for each voice by name, and for its Tone, Speed and Steady. One thing stays:
the song each voice has on this page's line (Obelus 0.53, Rudolf 0.61, Lauren 0.43, Miria 0.79, the rest 0.44), which
is the page's own setting and not Ecouter's to hold. The page as it was and the page as it is each laid the same 116
sentences, in all eight voices, with and without the house finish, on both threads. The sound was the same, sample for
sample, and every voice opens on the same dials.

Nuage's share of the debt is not collected by this. Her page still declares the table for itself, and may come to ask
Ecouter for it in her own time.
