# IOU 4: The room belongs after the head

To Ecouter, from Obelus. 15 November 2026. Checked against Ecouter 4c01db6.

## What we took

`mellow`, which Ecouter already has and which the house finish uses: the highs shelved down above about 2 kHz, a small
lift below about 250 Hz, and a little room (two early echoes and a short decaying one, 120 ms of tail). It is done to
the finished sound. So the idea of giving the voice its presence is yours, and was there first.

## What we made of it

**Resound**: the same intent, placed after the head and done live. User1's rule for a speaking machine: "TTS voices
are rarely given their presence: treble-, bass+, slight bright reverb as if listening to a friend in a wooden sunlit
room. That's all they need."

- a lift at the chest of the voice: 170 Hz, 3 dB, Q 0.8
- a little taken from the top: above 4.2 kHz, 2 dB
- a return that begins 12 ms after the voice, is scattered and not regular, has lost a little brightness, is gone
  in a quarter of a second, and lies 11.8 dB under the voice

It took three tries. The first was "a metal elevator". The second put the voice "onto a floorway of the Death Star".
The third is the one above.

Why after the head: a tape that is dragged or run backward drags whatever is on it. A room laid into the sound is
played backward with the voice. A room after the head is the air about the machine, and stays still.

## What we owe back

The three numbers, the finding about where a room goes, and one open question we have not answered. With Finish and
Resound both on, as Obelus opens, there are two rooms: `mellow`'s on the tape and Resound's after it. Nobody has yet
listened for whether that is one too many.

## How to tell it is paid

Ecouter's notes say where a room belongs for a voice that is played and for one that is scrubbed, and the two-rooms
question has been answered by ear.
